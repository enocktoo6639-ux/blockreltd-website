About this privacy policy

This draft describes the intended privacy practices of the BLOCK RE LTD corporate website. It will be finalized after hosting, business contact details and actual data handling practices have been verified. Separate policies will apply to individual applications.

01

Introduction

BLOCK RE LTD ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to respecting the privacy of visitors to its corporate website.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, disclosed and protected in connection with the website at blockreltd.online.

This policy is intended to help visitors understand the website's information handling practices and available privacy choices. It does not limit any rights provided by applicable data protection laws.

02

Information We Collect

Information you provide voluntarily

When you communicate with us through an official contact channel, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as:

  • Your name and email address.
  • The subject and contents of your enquiry.
  • Information necessary to respond to a support request.
  • Other details you voluntarily include in correspondence.

Technical information

Website hosting and network services may process technical information when a visitor accesses the website. This may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser and device information.
  • Requested pages and access timestamps.
  • Technical diagnostic and security information.

The current website design does not include user registration, payment processing, file uploads or an embedded contact submission form.

03

How We Use Information

Where relevant, information may be processed for legitimate website administration and communication purposes, including:

  • Operating and maintaining the corporate website.
  • Responding to enquiries and support requests.
  • Providing requested company or product information.
  • Investigating technical problems and security incidents.
  • Preventing unauthorized access, misuse or abuse.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

Information handling practices will be reviewed when additional website functionality is introduced.

04

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis for processing personal information, the relevant basis will depend on the nature and purpose of the processing.

Potential legal bases include:

  • Consent: Where permission is required and has been obtained.
  • Contractual necessity: Where processing is necessary to perform a contract or take requested steps before entering into a contract.
  • Legitimate interests: Where permitted for website administration, security and communication, subject to applicable safeguards.
  • Legal obligations: Where processing is required by applicable law.
05

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The current corporate website source code does not intentionally install advertising cookies, analytics trackers, tracking pixels or browser storage identifiers.

Infrastructure and hosting providers may separately process technical information or use technologies necessary for their services.

If analytics, advertising, optional cookies or other tracking technologies are added in the future, this policy will be updated and appropriate consent mechanisms will be implemented where required.

Visitors may manage cookies through their browser settings.

06

Information Sharing

Information may be disclosed where necessary for legitimate operational purposes and where permitted by applicable law.

Potential recipients include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Email and communication service providers.
  • Professional advisers where necessary.
  • Authorities where disclosure is legally required.

The current corporate website is not designed to sell visitor information or facilitate targeted advertising. Any material change to these practices will require a review of this policy.

07

Third-Party Services

Third-party infrastructure services may support website hosting, domain registration, DNS resolution, security and business communications.

GitHub

GitHub Pages is the intended hosting platform for this website. Visitors can learn about GitHub's information handling practices in its General Privacy Statement .

Namecheap

Namecheap is the domain registrar for the corporate website. Information about its privacy practices is available in the Namecheap Privacy Policy .

External websites

External websites linked from our pages may operate under their own privacy policies. Visitors should review the relevant policies before providing personal information to those websites.

08

Data Security

BLOCK RE LTD aims to implement reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information processed.

Planned safeguards include encrypted HTTPS connections, protected administrative accounts, appropriate access controls and responsible information handling.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security. Visitors should avoid sending passwords, financial credentials or other unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary email correspondence.

09

Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Relevant considerations may include:

  • The time required to respond to an enquiry.
  • Ongoing customer support requirements.
  • Applicable legal record-keeping obligations.
  • Security, dispute resolution and legitimate operational needs.

Third-party providers may apply separate retention periods to technical logs and service records. Specific company retention arrangements will be confirmed before this policy is finalized.

10

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Object to processing in applicable circumstances.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

Privacy requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law. Reasonable identity verification may be required to protect personal information and prevent unauthorized disclosure.

Official privacy request contact details will be provided in the Contact Information section when the business email service is operational.

11

Children's Privacy

This corporate website is intended to provide general company information and is not specifically directed toward children.

The current website design does not include account registration or features designed to solicit personal information from children.

If information concerning a child is received through correspondence, it should be handled in accordance with applicable children's privacy and data protection requirements.

Individual mobile applications may have different intended audiences and privacy requirements.

12

International Data Transfers

Website infrastructure and communication providers may operate in countries other than the country from which a visitor accesses the website.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, applicable data protection requirements and appropriate safeguards must be considered.

Details of relevant providers and transfer arrangements will be reviewed as part of the final website setup.

13

Mobile Applications and Digital Products

This policy applies specifically to the BLOCK RE LTD corporate website. It does not automatically describe the privacy practices of every mobile application or digital product that BLOCK RE LTD may develop or publish.

Individual applications require privacy disclosures appropriate to their actual functionality and information handling practices.

Where applicable, application-specific policies will address:

  • Personal information and device data collected.
  • Application permissions and their purposes.
  • Advertising, analytics and third-party SDKs.
  • Account management and data deletion.
  • Data sharing, retention and security practices.
  • Applicable audience and children's privacy requirements.

Relevant application policies will be made available through appropriate product pages and app store listings when those applications are published.

14

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in website functionality, information handling practices, service providers or applicable legal requirements.

The finalized policy will include an effective date and will be maintained on this page.

Where required by applicable law, material changes will be communicated through appropriate notices or consent mechanisms.

15

Contact Information

For questions concerning this policy, personal information handling or privacy rights, please refer to the organization's official contact channels.
